What's happening with the truck

The repair plan, in order.

1

Find cylinder 2's misfire

The check-engine light flashed during the road test. The spark plug was swapped with cylinder 4 and the misfire stayed on cylinder 2. The coil and boot have not been confirmed swapped. Check them, then the injector, wiring and compression as needed. Avoid more loaded driving while it's misfiring.

2

Confirm converter damage

Code P0430 points to low catalyst efficiency on bank 2. Tim also heard a rattle when checking the converters. Confirm both units are damaged and check for exhaust leaks before ordering two replacements.

3

Keep an eye on the throttle body

Code P2112 means the throttle was reported stuck closed. The FORScan drive log looked normal, but an intermittent issue can return. Ford bulletin 16-0139 may apply depending on build date and other codes.

4

Install parts, then check smog readiness

After the misfire is repaired and the right converters are available, install the needed parts, clear eligible faults, finish the readiness monitors, and confirm it is ready for a legitimate California smog inspection.

Questions that might come up

Why not just buy the $309.99 converters?

They are listed for Federal/EPA use and are not approved for California. Shipping them to Arizona does not change California installation and smog requirements. California BAR guidance.

Does the P0430 code mean both converters are bad?

No. The November 7 diagnostic report showed P0430 for bank 2, and did not show a current P0420 for bank 1. The reported rattling suggests physical damage, but both converters should be checked before spending money on the pair.

What is the Ford throttle-body bulletin?

Ford TSB 16-0139 addresses certain intermittent P2112 throttle-body faults on vehicles including the 2016 Expedition built on or before May 31, 2016. It has specific diagnostic conditions and exclusions. Jen's build date has not been verified here; the bulletin does not explain the cylinder 2 misfire by itself.
