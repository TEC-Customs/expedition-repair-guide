01 / Diagnose first

Get cylinder 2 firing reliably.

The saved FORScan report records P0302: cylinder 2 misfired. Tim reports that the misfire stayed on cylinder 2 after swapping the plug, boot and coil. The coil connector has damaged plastic; that makes it worth checking, but doesn’t prove it caused the misfire.

Next check: Inspect the connector lock, terminals and wiring for a loose contact. If spark is reliable, compare compression with the other cylinders and test injector operation. No compression or injector result is confirmed.

Before fitting converters: Verify the repair under the conditions that triggered the miss. Check cylinder misfire counts and pending codes; a light that goes out by itself isn’t enough.

02 / Separate throttle fault

Replace the throttle body as planned.

P2112 means the computer detected the throttle stuck closed. Cleaning stopped the reported symptoms, and the earlier FORScan test did not reproduce sticking. Neither rules out an intermittent fault.

When Ford bulletin 16-0139 applies

The bulletin covers specified vehicles and engines built on or before May 31, 2016, with intermittent power loss or a wrench light and stored P2111, P2112 or P2119. The service procedure also screens for other throttle-circuit codes that require separate diagnosis. Tim confirmed the build cutoff; the prior repair review reported P2112 without exclusion codes. Throttle-body replacement is the plan, not a verified fix for P0302. Read the Ford bulletin summary.

Two converters, two gaskets.

P0430 means catalyst efficiency measured below threshold on bank 2. It does not establish that both converters failed. Once the misfire is fixed, check for exhaust leaks and review oxygen-sensor readings and the catalyst monitor before committing to the quoted pair.

California fitment

For California, use the correct original-equipment converter or a CARB-approved aftermarket converter whose Executive Order covers this exact vehicle, engine family and installation position. A Federal/EPA-only listing is insufficient, even if the pipe fits. Have the shop check the underhood emissions label and confirm the application before ordering.
