The throttle body is the planned repair. One cylinder also sometimes misfires, and we’re checking what causes it.

Why replace the throttle body?

The throttle body controls how much air enters the engine when you press the gas pedal. Your Expedition recorded P2112, which means the computer detected the throttle sticking closed. When that happens, the truck can lose power.

The build date matches Ford’s service bulletin. The throttle opened during the short test drive, which can happen when a fault comes and goes.

What we’re checking on cylinder 2

A misfire means a cylinder occasionally misses a beat. The misfire stayed on cylinder 2 after its spark plug and ignition coil were swapped. We still need to find out why.

Sunday, Nov. 8 · Check the spark plug boots

Look for cracks or dark burn tracks in the rubber boots that carry the spark to the plugs. A damaged boot can let the spark escape.

Monday, Nov. 9 after work · Test compression

Bring the compression tester and compare cylinder 2 with the others. This checks whether it can build pressure. If it reads low, the next step is to find where pressure escapes. If it reads normally, the next checks are spark control and fuel delivery to that cylinder.

If the check-engine light keeps flashing or the engine shakes, pull over safely and arrange a tow.
